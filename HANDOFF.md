- **⚠ DESIGN ISSUE for non-leaf rewrites:** PS2Recomp switches guest threads by **unwinding** (`GuestThread` holds only an `R5900Context`; `dispatchGuestBranch` checks `checkpointDue()` on every call; a blocked `WaitSema` also unwinds).
